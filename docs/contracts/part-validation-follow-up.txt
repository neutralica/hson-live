P3 follow-up: incremental validation of governed Parts

Adding a new name to an existing Parts collection still validates and reparses
the complete catalog. Composition reads and .write() also repeat catalog validation.
This is a nonblocking engineering issue, much of which predates mandatory
immutability. Examine incremental validation and reuse of established immutable
definitions separately, preserving atomic admission and authority checks.

The SSR subject assertion repair makes no production, validator, cache, or
transition-planner changes.
